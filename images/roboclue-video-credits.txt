The opening uses real-robot execution footage from:
DexMimicGen: Automated Data Generation for Bimanual Dexterous Manipulation via Imitation Learning.
Authors: Zhenyu Jiang, Yuqi Xie, Kevin Lin, Zhenjia Xu, Weikang Wan,
Ajay Mandlekar, Linxi Fan, and Yuke Zhu.
Affiliations: NVIDIA Research, UT Austin, and UC San Diego.
Project and source-license statement: https://dexmimicgen.github.io/
Original video: https://dexmimicgen.github.io/resources/real2sim2real.mp4

Changes made in the RoboClue adaptation: the 39-42 second real-robot excerpt
is cropped to remove the bottom 104-pixel caption band, resampled to 30 fps,
fitted to a square canvas, and overlaid with RoboClue captions. The three-second
excerpt spans a five-second opening at 60% of its published rate, resulting in
6x playback from the original 10x footage. No synthetic robot footage or AI
resolution enhancement is used.
